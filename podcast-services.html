Riverside Recording Studio

Your podcast. Our recording studio.

Don't just record a call. Produce a show.

Through our Riverside recording studio, you can record your podcast remotely while we manage the technical side.

You focus on hosting. We're behind the scenes checking the equipment, monitoring the recording, helping guests get settled and catching technical issues before they become editing nightmares.

Whether you're recording solo, interviewing an author or hosting a co-hosted show, you don't have to figure out the production side alone.

What's included in supported recording?

  • Your own scheduled Riverside recording session.
  • Guest invitations and joining instructions.
  • Microphone, camera and headphone checks.
  • Live producer monitoring.
  • Technical troubleshooting during the session.
  • Recording and upload checks.
  • Raw recording files available for editing or delivery.

No need to manage the recording software or worry about whether everyone is recording properly. We help take that off your plate.

FAQ

Do I need to buy equipment before contacting you?

No. Start with the equipment you already own. Coaching can help you decide what, if anything, to upgrade.

Can I record through your Riverside studio?

Yes. Our supported remote recording services let you book a session through our Riverside studio with live producer support.

Will you host the interview for me?

Our recording packages provide technical production while you host. Guest sourcing, research, interviewing and scripting are separate custom scopes, not standard inclusions.

Can I just send you my existing recording?

Yes. We offer standalone audio and video editing for creators who already have their recordings.

Will you publish my episode?

Not unless publishing is specifically included in your quote. Standard editing and recording packages deliver files for you to approve and publish.

What if my episode is longer or has more guests?

Tell us before booking. We will confirm a custom scope and price.
